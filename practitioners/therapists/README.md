## Structural findings

- **France — institutionally asymmetric, not merely numerically split**: NO French *autorité publique* (HAS, Académie nationale de médecine, SFPEADA, CNPE) has ever endorsed SAP. MIVILUDES has repeatedly flagged it (vigilance signals in annual reports — note: NOT a "ban" as often paraphrased). Recognition camp rests on ~3 clinicians (Bensussan / Delfieu / Biolley) plus one NGO (ACALPA / Odinetz), with **zero university chair, zero société savante endorsement**. CIIVISE 2023 report + loi n° 2024-233 + November 2024 non-reappointment of Édouard Durand form a single political sequence that hardened critique-camp institutional position while politicising its personnel — **without restoring legitimacy to SAP**. Recognition 5 / Critique 6 / Middle 3 / Forensic-evaluator 3.
- **Brazil and Spain are the world's only two statutory PA jurisdictions — pointed in opposite directions**. Brazil's Lei 12.318/2010 codifies *alienação parental* as actionable; Spain's LOPIVI (Ley Orgánica 8/2021) Disposición adicional decimoséptima inadmits *"el llamado síndrome de alienación parental"* by exact name.
- **Mexico — uniquely fragmented LATAM landscape**: Sociedad Mexicana de Psicología has issued NO public PA-specific institutional position. CDMX Código Civil art. 323 Septimus was **derogated 4 Aug 2017** (ALDF 36-0 unanimous); SCJN **AI 11/2016 (Oaxaca)** invalidated only the *automaticity* of suspensión/pérdida de patria potestad. AP survives in state-level codes (Edomex / Aguascalientes / Coahuila / Hidalgo / Querétaro / Puebla).
- **Spain — terminological-arbitrage gap**: LOPIVI targets only the SAP label by name. Recognition clinicians (Tejedor's PIVIP) shifted to *interferencias parentales* / *manipulación de la voluntad del menor*. No unified national COP position; per-CCAA variation opposite of DE/UK pattern.
- **Germany — institutional inflection 2023-2026**: BVerfG 17.11.2023 – 1 BvR 1076/23 + DJI Zimmermann et al. 2023 ZKJ + OLG Frankfurt 7 UF 88/25 (Jan 2026) moved critique camp from minority to institutional mainstream. See [BVerfG case study](../../case-studies/bverfg-1-bvr-1076-23-germany-2023.md) + [OLG Frankfurt case study](../../case-studies/olg-frankfurt-7-uf-88-25-germany-2026.md).
- **Brazil**: only LATAM jurisdiction where institutional critique pole (CFP NT 4/2022 + CFESS) and clinical recognition pole (IBDFAM-centred) are roughly equal in institutional weight.
- **India**: ZERO individual critique-camp clinicians with peer-reviewed PA-critique publication. Critique is institutional/womens-rights-org-led, not clinician-led.
