## Structural findings

- **Italy — strongest EU statutory-and-caselaw stance against PAS-CTU**: Cassazione I Civile ordinanze **13217/2021 → 9691/2022 → 4595/2025**, each binding judges to verify the scientific foundation of CTU and each recepting the 2012 Ministero della Salute note. Combined with **Riforma Cartabia (D.lgs. 149/2022, artt. 473-bis ss. c.p.c.)** which codifies that the CTU cannot adjudicate domestic violence, Italy is now the strictest EU jurisdiction by *statute + caselaw* against PAS-grounded CTU — stronger than Spain's STS 519/2017 because it is three apex rulings + a code reform rather than one decision. Critique anchored institutionally in CISMAI + GREVIO Italy 2020 + *Maltrattamento e Abuso all'Infanzia* editorial line. Recognition anchored in Torino-Forense (Gulotta) + SIPGI (Pingitore). Recognition 8 / Critique 5 / Middle 1 / Institutional 5.
- **Argentina — institutional inadmission without statutory inadmission (inverse of Spain)**: Argentina has no statute that names SAP/AP either to recognise or to inadmit. The institutional critique register is the densest in LATAM: FePRA Pronunciamiento 14.12.2019 + 7 provincial Colegios + ASAPMI + Defensoría del Pueblo CABA. Routed through *colegios profesionales* rather than a single federal regulator. The 2024 Milei restructuring shifted the institutional anchor from federal executive to universities + colegios + civil society. Recognition 3 / Critique 5 / Institutional 5 / Cross-jurisdictional 1.
- **Brazil and Spain are the world's only two statutory PA jurisdictions — pointed in opposite directions**. Brazil's Lei 12.318/2010 codifies *alienação parental* as actionable; Spain's LOPIVI inadmits *"el llamado síndrome de alienación parental"* by exact name.
- **France — institutionally asymmetric**: NO French *autorité publique* (HAS, Académie nationale de médecine, SFPEADA, CNPE) has ever endorsed SAP. Recognition camp rests on ~3 clinicians + 1 NGO (ACALPA) with zero university chair, zero société savante endorsement.
- **Germany — institutional inflection 2023-2026**: BVerfG 17.11.2023 – 1 BvR 1076/23 + DJI Zimmermann et al. 2023 ZKJ + OLG Frankfurt 7 UF 88/25 (Jan 2026). See [BVerfG case study](../../case-studies/bverfg-1-bvr-1076-23-germany-2023.md) + [OLG Frankfurt case study](../../case-studies/olg-frankfurt-7-uf-88-25-germany-2026.md).
- **Mexico — uniquely fragmented**: Sociedad Mexicana de Psicología has issued NO public PA-specific institutional position. CDMX Código Civil art. 323 Septimus was derogated 4 Aug 2017.
- **Spain — terminological-arbitrage gap**: LOPIVI targets only the SAP label by name. Recognition clinicians shifted to *interferencias parentales* / *manipulación de la voluntad del menor*.
- **Brazil**: only LATAM jurisdiction where institutional critique pole (CFP NT 4/2022 + CFESS) and clinical recognition pole (IBDFAM-centred) are roughly equal in institutional weight.
- **India**: ZERO individual critique-camp clinicians with peer-reviewed PA-critique publication.
