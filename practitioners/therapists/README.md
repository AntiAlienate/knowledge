## Structural findings

- **Brazil and Spain are the world's only two statutory PA jurisdictions — pointed in opposite directions**. Brazil's Lei 12.318/2010 codifies *alienação parental* as actionable; Spain's LOPIVI (Ley Orgánica 8/2021) Disposición adicional decimoséptima inadmits *"el llamado síndrome de alienación parental"* by exact name. No other national statute on either side.
- **Spain — terminological-arbitrage gap**: LOPIVI targets only the SAP label. Recognition clinicians (notably Tejedor's PIVIP) shifted terminology to *interferencias parentales* / *manipulación de la voluntad del menor* — language LOPIVI does not explicitly inadmit. The central post-2021 Spanish PA puzzle. **No unified national COP position exists**: COP Madrid recognition-tolerant, COPC middle, COP-Asturias forensic-empirical, COP Andalucía critique-leaning — opposite of the German/UK pattern. Critique 6 / Recognition 5 / Middle 2 / Institutional 3.
- **Germany — institutional inflection 2023-2026**: BVerfG 17.11.2023 – 1 BvR 1076/23 (2. Kammer des Ersten Senats, unanimous) held at Rn. 34 that the OLG Köln below relied on *"das überkommene und fachwissenschaftlich als widerlegt geltende Konzept des sogenannten Parental Alienation Syndrom (kurz PAS)"*. OLG decision annulled, remanded. Combined with DJI-anchored Zimmermann/Fichtner/Walper/Lux/Kindler 2023 ZKJ *"Verdorbener Wein in neuen Schläuchen"* critique (cited in the BVerfG decision itself) and OLG Frankfurt 7 UF 88/25 (Jan 2026) Sachverständigen-Mindestanforderungen, the German critique camp has moved from minority to institutional mainstream. See [case study](../../case-studies/bverfg-1-bvr-1076-23-germany-2023.md).
- **Brazil**: only LATAM jurisdiction where institutional critique pole (CFP NT 4/2022 + CFESS joint nota) and clinical recognition pole (IBDFAM-centred) are roughly equal in institutional weight.
- **India**: ZERO individual critique-camp clinicians with peer-reviewed PA-critique publication. Critique is institutional/womens-rights-org-led, not clinician-led.
