## Structural findings

- **Germany — institutional inflection 2023-2026**: BVerfG 17.11.2023 – 1 BvR 1076/23 (2. Kammer des Ersten Senats, unanimous) held at Rn. 34 that the OLG Köln below relied on "*das überkommene und fachwissenschaftlich als widerlegt geltende Konzept des sogenannten Parental Alienation Syndrom (kurz PAS)*" — translation: "*the outdated concept of so-called Parental Alienation Syndrome (PAS), which is regarded in the relevant scientific literature as refuted*". OLG decision annulled, remanded. Combined with DJI-anchored Zimmermann/Fichtner/Walper/Lux/Kindler 2023 ZKJ "*Verdorbener Wein in neuen Schläuchen*" critique (cited in the BVerfG decision itself) and OLG Frankfurt 7 UF 88/25 (Jan 2026) Sachverständigen-Mindestanforderungen, the German critique camp has moved from minority to institutional mainstream. Recognition camp is now counter-mainstream — inverse of most other PASG-active jurisdictions. Critique 7 / Recognition 6 / Middle 3. See [case study](../../case-studies/bverfg-1-bvr-1076-23-germany-2023.md).
- **Brazil**: only LATAM jurisdiction where institutional critique pole (CFP NT 4/2022 + CFESS joint nota) and clinical recognition pole (IBDFAM-centred Calçada / Glicia Brazil / Trindade / Molinari / Lobo Ribeiro–Karam) are roughly equal in institutional weight. Without CFP+CFESS the clinical-practitioner critique camp would be visibly thinner.
- **India**: ZERO individual critique-camp clinicians with peer-reviewed PA-critique publication. Critique is institutional/womens-rights-org-led, not clinician-led.
