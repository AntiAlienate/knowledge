# Africa Therapists & Clinicians — Parental Alienation Practitioner Directory

**Schema version:** 1.0
**Last updated:** 2026-05-25
**License:** CC BY 4.0 (intended for github.com/AntiAlienate/antialienate-knowledge)
**Region:** Africa (anchor jurisdiction **South Africa**; institutional entries for Kenya, Nigeria, Egypt)
**Scope:** Africa-based clinical / counselling / forensic / educational psychologists, psychiatric social workers, parenting coordinators and clinical social workers who have (a) been court-appointed expert, curator-clinical, facilitator or parenting coordinator of record in reported PA litigation, (b) published peer-reviewed or book-length material on parental alienation in an African journal / SciELO / J. Divorce & Remarriage, (c) hold an HPCSA / SACSSP / Counsellors-and-Psychologists-Board-(Kenya) / NCNP-Nigeria registration AND publish a sourced PA stance, or (d) lead a national institutional body with a sourced PA position. Institutional entries (PAFSA, Childline SA, SACSSP, NCCM Egypt) listed where the institution itself has issued a sourced position or programmatic frame.

**Standards:** Primary sources only — HPCSA / SACSSP register entries where searchable, practice / institutional website, SAFLII / Kenya Law / Egyptian official gazette judgments, peer-reviewed publications (Social Work/Maatskaplike Werk, PER/PELJ, J. Divorce & Remarriage), named press (Mail & Guardian, TimesLIVE, MedicalBrief, Intercept-equivalent). Where a fact could not be confirmed from a public primary source it is flagged "**not publicly confirmed**".

**Stance taxonomy:**
- `recognition` — treats parental alienation as a real, assessable clinical phenomenon; conducts PA-specific assessment, facilitation, parenting-coordination or reunification work; typically PASG / Family-Bridges-aligned or aligned with the SA recognition-camp jurisprudence anchored in T.L.D v B.G.
- `critique` — argues PA construct (especially Gardner's SAP) is operationalised against DV / GBV survivors (predominantly mothers and children); advocates DV/trauma-informed alternatives, child-voice safeguards and tighter regulation of expert witnesses.
- `middle` — engages both alienating-behaviour and family-violence frames; differential-diagnosis or systemic / multi-family approach; parenting-coordination / mediation focus; not aligned with PASG and not in the abolitionist critique camp.
- `not_publicly_confirmed` — included for institutional record completeness; PA-specific stance not in primary sources.

**Critical Africa regulatory & policy context (must read before using this directory):**

- **South Africa (ZA):** Psychologists register with the **Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA)** — Professional Board for Psychology (https://www.hpcsa.co.za/). Categories: Clinical, Counselling, Educational, Industrial, Research, Forensic. Social workers register with the **South African Council for Social Service Professions (SACSSP)** under the Social Service Professions Act 110 of 1978 (https://www.sacssp.co.za/). **Parenting coordinators** are accredited via **NABFAM** (https://nabfam.co.za/) — a standing committee of the Africa Centre for Dispute Settlement at the University of Stellenbosch Business School — and via voluntary associations FAMAC (Cape; https://famac.co.za/), KAFAM (KZN), SAAM (national), Family Life Centre, Social Justice Association of Mediators. **Mediation Standards of Practice for South Africa** drafted 2016; **SA Standards of Practice for Forensic Assessments** drafted 2021 (task force chaired by Dr Astrid Martalas). The leading SA judicial citation on PA-as-litigable harm is **T.L.D v B.G** (015642/2022) [2023] ZAGPJHC 801 (Adams J, Gauteng Local Division Johannesburg, 13 July 2023), with named clinical expert **Leonard Carr** and parenting coordinators **Dr Lynette Roux** + **Adv Vicky Olivier** and father's expert **Christie Els** — all four are entries here. Children's Act 38 of 2005 is the statutory frame.
- **Kenya (KE):** Psychologists register with the **Counsellors and Psychologists Board (CPRB)** under the Counsellors and Psychologists Act 2014 (https://cprb.ecitizen.go.ke/). Two parallel professional associations: **Kenya Counselling and Psychological Association (KCPA)** since 1988/1990 (~7,000 members; https://kenyacounsellingandpsychologicalassociation.org/) and **Kenya Psychological Association (KPsyA)** (https://kpsya.or.ke/). The Children Act 2022 codifies best-interests-of-the-child as paramount in custody. No institutional PA position statement publicly issued by either KPsyA or KCPA within the time-budget public-web search.
- **Nigeria (NG):** Psychologists register with the **Nigeria Association of Clinical Psychologists (NACP)** (https://nacp.com.ng/) and licensure under the Nigerian Psychological Association (NPA). No federal PA-specific licensing regime; family-court PA jurisprudence anchored in Matrimonial Causes Act + Child's Rights Act under best-interests standard. No public PA-position statement from NACP / NPA located within the time budget.
- **Egypt (EG):** Psychologists licensed via the Ministry of Health and the Egyptian Psychiatric Association (psychiatrists). The state body engaging custody-and-access reform is the **National Council for Childhood and Motherhood (NCCM)** (https://hrightsstudies.sis.gov.eg/en/bodies/councils/local/the-national-council-for-childhood-and-motherhood/) — established 1988 by Presidential Decree 54/1988. NCCM Child Helpline 16000 (66,645 calls Jan-Feb 2025) provides psychological support and family counselling; Safe Child Unit pilot across six governorates (announced Jan 2026). No public Arabic-language PA-position statement publicly issued by NCCM or by the Egyptian Psychiatric Association within the time-budget search.

## South Africa

The SA clinical / forensic PA terrain is anchored by the four named-of-record experts from **T.L.D v B.G [2023] ZAGPJHC 801** (Leonard Carr, Dr Lynette Roux, Adv Vicky Olivier, Christie Els) plus the SA Standards of Practice for Forensic Assessments task-force chair **Dr Astrid Martalas** (Cape Town, 300+ court appointments) and the Centurion clinical psychologist **Dr Marlé Viljoen** whose 2014 *Journal of Divorce and Remarriage* paper with van Rensburg is the standard SA peer-reviewed clinical citation on PA. The North-West University social-work cluster (**Bosch-Brits / Wessels / Roux 2018**, *Social Work/Maatskaplike Werk*) supplies the SACSSP-side qualitative evidence base. Institutional poles: **PAFSA (Parental Alienation Foundation of South Africa)** co-founded by attorney Howard Watson is the recognition-camp institutional advocacy body; **Childline SA** and former Childline national coordinator **Joan van Niekerk** (now KZN Children's Hospital, ISPCAN past President) anchor the broader child-protection institutional frame within which PA is now litigated. There is no SA equivalent of CFP Brazil's NT 4/2022 — neither HPCSA nor SACSSP has issued a PA-position notice as at the time of this directory.
