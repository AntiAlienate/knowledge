## Scope and inclusion test

India-based clinical psychologists, psychiatric social workers, child and adolescent psychiatrists, family therapists, and court-attached counsellors who satisfy at least one of the following — verified from primary sources only:

- **(a)** have authored or co-authored peer-reviewed work explicitly framing parental alienation, parental alienation syndrome (PAS), or parental alienating behaviours as a clinical / child-protection phenomenon (Indian Journal of Psychological Medicine, International Journal of Social Psychiatry, Asian Journal of Psychiatry, Journal of Indian Association for Child & Adolescent Mental Health, etc.); or
- **(b)** have been named on the record as a court-appointed counsellor, custody evaluator, or expert witness in a reported Indian family-court / High Court / Supreme Court judgment that turned on PA / alienating behaviour findings; or
- **(c)** operate or direct a PA-relevant reunification, co-parenting, or high-conflict family intervention through a named institution (NIMHANS-SAMVAD, IHBAS, AIIMS, Bombay HC pre-litigation counselling centre, Family Justice Courts); or
- **(d)** hold a publicly stated PA stance in named press, conference proceedings, or a primary-source practice-site article series.

**Stance is sourced, not inferred.** Practitioners with practice-site claims but no PA-specific primary source are listed in the Excluded / Not-yet-verified section below for transparency.

## India PA legal-regulatory context

- **No single national psychology licensing register.** The **Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI)**, constituted under the *Rehabilitation Council of India Act 1992*, registers Clinical Psychologists via the Central Rehabilitation Register (CRR) — https://www.rehabcouncil.nic.in/ . Lookup is by name/state; CRR numbers are not enumerated in bulk on the public site.
- **Psychiatric Social Work is not statutorily registered**; credentialing flows through academic institutions (NIMHANS PSW, TISS) and the Indian Society of Professional Social Work.
- **Psychiatry** is regulated under the **National Medical Commission Act 2019** (formerly Medical Council of India Act 1956); MD Psychiatry / DPM credentials and Indian Medical Register entries are searchable at https://www.nmc.org.in/information-desk/indian-medical-register/ .
- **NIMHANS Bengaluru** (deemed University under the **NIMHANS Act 2012**) is the apex training institute and de facto national reference centre for child mental-health forensic / custody work. **IHBAS Delhi** (autonomous body, established 1993 pursuant to Supreme Court PIL) is the apex North-India neuro-psychiatric institute and RCI-recognised M.Phil Clinical Psychology trainer.
- **Apex PA-recognition judgment: Lt Col Vivek Singh v Romani Singh, Civil Appeal 3962/2016, (2017) 3 SCC 231** — Bench: Jasti Chelameswar J. and A.K. Sikri J., decided 13 February 2017. Judgment PDF: https://main.sci.gov.in/judgment/judis/44566.pdf . IndianKanoon: https://indiankanoon.org/doc/48721287/ . The Court expressly defined "The Parental Alienation Syndrome" and identified two destructive effects: (i) placing the child in a loyalty contest causing guilt and confusion, and (ii) requiring the child to shift reality assessment, presenting one parent as "totally to blame" and "devoid of any positive characteristics." Court-appointed evaluator named on the record: **Ms. Iti Kanungo, Principal Counsellor, Family Court Patiala House, New Delhi** (report dated 12 December 2016) — see Entry 8 below.
- **Subsequent High Court PA-affirming line:** *ABC v XYZ*, 2023 SCC OnLine Del 6099 (Delhi HC, Suresh Kumar Kait J. and Neena Bansal Krishna J., October 2023) holding parental alienation from a non-neglectful parent constitutes an "extreme act of mental cruelty" under HMA s.13(1)(ia) — https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2023/10/01/delhi-hc-alienation-of-the-child-from-his-father-is-an-extreme-act-of-mental-cruelty-legal-news/ ; *A v B*, 2026 SCC OnLine Del 276 (Delhi HC, Anil Kshetarpal J. and Harish Vaidyanathan Shankar J., February 2026) upholding transfer of custody on findings of "sustained parental alienation" by the mother — https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/02/02/del-hc-custody-to-father-parental-alienation/ . **No named mental-health expert appears on the record in either 2023 or 2026 judgment.**
