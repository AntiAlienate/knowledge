## Jurisdictional Context

Germany is one of the most contested PA-clinical jurisdictions in continental Europe. Any clinician practising in this space must be read against six load-bearing legal anchors:

1. **BGB § 1684** — Both parents are obliged to maintain contact (*Umgang*) with the child; the parent with whom the child lives must promote the relationship with the other parent (the *Wohlverhaltensgebot* / loyalty clause).
2. **BGB § 1696** — Family-court rulings on custody and contact are modifiable for *triftige, das Wohl des Kindes nachhaltig berührende Gründe* (weighty reasons sustainably affecting the child's welfare).
3. **FamFG § 159** — Mandatory personal hearing of the child (*Kindesanhörung*) in family proceedings; the procedural standard German courts use to weigh the *Kindeswille*.
4. **BGH 1980 / 2009 Umgangsausschluss line** — Total contact-exclusion is the *ultima ratio*; the BGH requires that less restrictive measures (supervised contact, *Umgangspflegschaft*) be exhausted first.
5. **Cochemer Modell (Familiengericht Cochem-Zell, 1992, Richter Jürgen Rudolph)** — Interdisciplinary cooperation of court, Jugendamt, family-counselling and psychologists, with rapid first hearing; core elements absorbed into the 2009 *FamFG* reform. ([Wikipedia – Cochemer Modell](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cochemer_Modell))
6. **BVerfG 17 November 2023 — 1 BvR 1076/23** — The constitutional pivot. The First Senate's 1st Chamber held that decisions transferring custody substantially based on PAS / "Eltern-Kind-Entfremdung" rest on a *"wissenschaftlich überholten und widerlegten"* (scientifically outdated and refuted) concept; only an all-circumstances, child-welfare-centred weighing is constitutional. ([BVerfG decision text](https://www.bundesverfassungsgericht.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/2023/11/rk20231117_1bvr107623.html))

**Istanbul Convention art. 31** (consideration of incidents of violence in custody / contact decisions) and the **GREVIO 2022 Germany evaluation** add the international-law overlay. GREVIO criticised the continued use of "scientifically refuted concepts such as parental alienation … to the detriment of women" in German family proceedings — the central frame around which the *critique camp* below is organised. ([Frauenhauskoordinierung GREVIO press](https://www.frauenhauskoordinierung.de/aktuelles/detail/pressemeldung-zum-grevio-bericht-deutschland-braucht-gesamtkonzept-gegen-gewalt-an-frauen))

**Regulation of practitioners:**
- *Approbation* as **Ärztliche/r Psychotherapeut:in** or **Psychologische/r Psychotherapeut:in** (state licence, since 2020 *PsychThG* reform issued to graduates of the new direct study path).
- Chamber registration with the Land **Psychotherapeutenkammer** (e.g. PTK Berlin, LPK BW, PTK Bayern).
- Forensic *Sachverständige* in family-court matters are typically **Fachpsycholog:innen für Rechtspsychologie BDP/DGPs** or **Diplom-Psycholog:innen** with rechtspsychologische Zusatzqualifikation; recognised credentials are listed by the *Sektion Rechtspsychologie* of the BDP. ([BDP Sektion Rechtspsychologie](https://www.rechtspsychologie-bdp.de/aktuelles/))

**Note on direction of travel:** Since 2022 the German clinical mainstream — DJI, BMFSFJ-funded research and the *Frauenhauskoordinierung*-aligned NGOs — has moved decisively against the PAS/EKE construct, culminating in the 2023 BVerfG chamber decision. The recognition camp (Boch-Galhau, Kodjoe, Rücker, the *Verband für Getrennterziehen* circle) remains active but is now **counter-mainstream** rather than central.

## Forensic Sachverständige — Repeatedly appointed in OLG case-law

**OLG Frankfurt am Main, 7 UF 88/25 (decision dated 05.01.2026)** — leading 2026 ruling. The Senate affirmed the BVerfG line, characterised PAS as *"pseudowissenschaftliche Hypothese"*, and set out **minimum methodological requirements** for usable Sachverständigen-Gutachten in PA-allegation cases: multimodal data sources, no restriction to one-sidedly selected discussion partners ([anwalt.de case note](https://www.anwalt.de/rechtstipps/sorgerechtsentzug-nach-1666-bgb-wegen-elterlicher-konflikte-olg-frankfurt-zieht-klare-grenzen-268772.html), [rechtundpolitik.com case note](https://www.rechtundpolitik.com/justiz/oberlandesgericht-frankfurt-am-main/umgangsverweigerung-eines-kindes-nicht-automatisch-auf-manipulation-zurueckzufuehren/), [urteile.news](https://urteile.news/OLG-Frankfurt-am-Main__Umgangsverweigerung-eines-Kindes-zu-einem-Elternteil-kann-ohne-konkrete-Anhaltspunkte-nicht-auf-unbewusste-Beeinflussung-des-Kindes-durch-anderen-Elternteil~N35695)). The *unnamed Sachverständige* in this case had recommended a forced custody-transfer based on alleged maternal alienation and was effectively repudiated by the Senate.
