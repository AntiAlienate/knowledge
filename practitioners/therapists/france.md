## Jurisdictional Context

France is one of the most institutionally contested PA-clinical jurisdictions in continental Europe. The construct *aliénation parentale* / *syndrome d'aliénation parentale* (SAP) is **not recognised as a diagnostic category** by any French public-health authority, and has been the object of repeated official warnings. Any clinician practising in this space must be read against eight load-bearing anchors:

1. **Code civil art. 372-2-1 / 373-2 / 373-2-11** — the *juge aux affaires familiales* (JAF) has wide discretion on residence (*résidence*) and contact (*droit de visite et d'hébergement*, DVH). Art. 373-2-11 enumerates non-exhaustive criteria the JAF "prend notamment en considération" (notably takes into account), including the child's feelings (*sentiments exprimés par l'enfant mineur*), and the aptitude of each parent to assume their duties and to respect the rights of the other. ([Légifrance — art. 373-2-11](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000020433058))
2. **Code civil art. 373-2-6 al. 3 + loi du 18 mars 2024** — JAF may order a *mesure judiciaire d'aide à la gestion du budget familial*, supervised contact in a *espace de rencontre*, or a *médiation familiale*; the 2024 *loi visant à mieux protéger et accompagner les enfants victimes et covictimes de violences intrafamiliales* (loi n° 2024-233) explicitly tightened the framework where intrafamily violence is alleged. ([Légifrance — loi n° 2024-233](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000049280040))
3. **MIVILUDES — Mission interministérielle de vigilance et de lutte contre les dérives sectaires.** The MIVILUDES *Rapport annuel* 2006 and subsequent commentaries flagged *SAP* as a concept used in the context of family disputes that warranted vigilance, particularly given its non-recognition by mainstream psychiatry and its instrumentalisation in custody litigation. MIVILUDES has never endorsed SAP as a clinical entity. ([MIVILUDES rapports](https://www.miviludes.interieur.gouv.fr/publications/rapports-au-premier-ministre))
4. **HAS — Haute Autorité de Santé.** HAS has issued **no clinical guideline** (*recommandation de bonne pratique*) endorsing SAP / aliénation parentale as a diagnostic category. The 2014 HAS *Repérage et signalement de l'inceste par les médecins* recommendation, and the 2025 HAS work on *Repérage des violences faites aux enfants*, treat the welfare of the child without invoking SAP. ([HAS — recommandations](https://www.has-sante.fr/jcms/c_1067136/fr/reperage-et-signalement-de-l-inceste-par-les-medecins-reconnaitre-les-maltraitances-sexuelles-intrafamiliales-chez-le-mineur))
5. **CIIVISE 2023 — Commission indépendante sur l'inceste et les violences sexuelles faites aux enfants.** The 17 November 2023 final report *Violences sexuelles faites aux enfants : on vous croit* devotes a developed section to the misuse of *aliénation parentale* allegations as a defensive technique deployed against mothers who disclose paternal sexual or physical violence; the report calls for the construct to be excluded from family-court expertise. ([CIIVISE rapport 2023](https://www.ciivise.fr/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/CIIVISE-RAPPORT-2023.pdf))
6. **CIIVISE post-2024 controversy.** In autumn 2024 the *Commission indépendante sur l'inceste et les violences sexuelles faites aux enfants* was reconstituted under Sébastien Boueilh; **juge Édouard Durand was not reappointed as co-président**, a decision contested publicly by survivor associations and many of the original commissioners and read by part of the field as a political signal regarding the CIIVISE's PA-critical line. ([Le Monde 19 nov. 2024](https://www.lemonde.fr/societe/article/2024/11/19/ciivise-l-evincement-du-juge-edouard-durand-suscite-un-tolle_6403101_3224.html), [Public Sénat coverage](https://www.publicsenat.fr/actualites/societe/inceste-le-juge-edouard-durand-ecarte-de-la-ciivise-une-decision-qui-passe-mal))
7. **CRIP — Cellule de recueil des informations préoccupantes.** Each département operates a CRIP under the President of the Conseil départemental (Code de l'action sociale et des familles art. L226-3); it is the routine first-line receiver of *informations préoccupantes* when *aliénation parentale* is alleged in either direction. Décret n° 2013-994 + Loi du 14 mars 2016 *de protection de l'enfant* shape its protocol.
8. **SFPEADA — Société Française de Psychiatrie de l'Enfant et de l'Adolescent et des Disciplines Associées** + **Académie nationale de médecine.** The SFPEADA has consistently declined to endorse SAP as a diagnosis; the Académie nationale de médecine has not issued any *avis* validating the construct. By contrast the recognition-camp NGO **ACALPA** (Association Contre l'Aliénation Parentale) is the central French civil-society anchor for the PA-recognition position. ([SFPEADA](http://www.sfpeada.fr/), [Académie nationale de médecine](https://www.academie-medecine.fr/), [ACALPA](https://acalpa.info/))

**Regulation of practitioners:**
- *Psychiatre* / *pédopsychiatre*: doctor of medicine + DES de psychiatrie or DES + option *psychiatrie de l'enfant et de l'adolescent*; registration with the *Conseil national de l'Ordre des médecins* (CNOM).
- *Psychologue*: title protected by loi n° 85-772 of 25 July 1985; ADELI / RPPS number; *Code de déontologie des psychologues* 2021. Professional federation: **FFPP — Fédération Française des Psychologues et de Psychologie**.
- *Psychothérapeute*: title protected by loi HPST 2009 + décret n° 2010-534; ARS registration.
- *Expert judiciaire*: inscription sur la liste de la Cour d'appel (Loi n° 71-498 modifiée; décret n° 2004-1463) — *expert près la Cour d'appel de…* or, more rarely, *expert agréé par la Cour de cassation*.

**Note on direction of travel:** Since the CIIVISE 2023 report and the 2024 *loi violences intrafamiliales*, the French clinical-institutional mainstream has hardened against the SAP construct. The recognition camp (Bensussan, Delfieu, the ACALPA network) remains visible and continues to publish, but is now **counter-mainstream** in the sense that no Ordre / société savante / autorité publique has moved toward formal endorsement. The September–November 2024 eviction of juge Édouard Durand from the reconstituted CIIVISE is the single most consequential recent inflection point and reshaped the political terrain — without, however, restoring institutional legitimacy to SAP.

## Structural Finding Summary

The French parental-alienation practitioner landscape diverges sharply from the German, Spanish or Italian patterns documented elsewhere in this directory, in three structural ways:

1. **No French *autorité publique* has ever endorsed SAP.** Neither HAS, the Académie nationale de médecine, the SFPEADA, nor the CNPE has issued a guideline or avis recognising *aliénation parentale* as a diagnostic category. By contrast, MIVILUDES has on multiple occasions issued vigilance signals. This is a stronger institutional asymmetry than in Germany (where the BVerfG 2023 chamber decision arrived against a more balanced clinical literature) and far stronger than in Spain or Brazil.

2. **The 2023 CIIVISE rapport reframed the debate from "is SAP scientifically valid?" to "how is SAP used in court?"** — and answered: as a defensive technique against DV-disclosing mothers. The 2024 *loi violences intrafamiliales* (n° 2024-233) and the November 2024 eviction of juge Édouard Durand from the reconstituted CIIVISE form a single political sequence whose net effect is to *harden* the critique-camp position institutionally while *politicising* the personnel who carried it.

3. **The recognition camp in France is unusually narrow.** It rests on a small number of named clinical voices (Bensussan, Delfieu, Biolley) and one NGO (ACALPA). It has no university chair, no société savante endorsement, and no HAS recommendation. By contrast, the critique camp is institutionally embedded across the CIIVISE, the *Délégation aux droits des femmes*, the MIPROF, the CNPE, Université Paris Nanterre (Gruev-Vintila), the *victimologie* clinical-academic field (Lopez, Sadlier, Salmona), and the *médecine légale* community (Daligand). The asymmetry is institutional, not merely numerical.

A neutral practitioner directory must therefore present the camps as *clinically symmetrical positions* while flagging the *institutional asymmetry* clearly. The 17 entries above attempt that balance: 5 recognition-camp (incl. 1 NGO, 1 nuanced bridge), 6 critique-camp (incl. 1 cross-border anchor), 3 middle-camp (incl. 1 francophone cross-border), 3 forensic-evaluator (Cour d'appel inscribed) — anchored by 10 institutional bodies and a CIIVISE-2024 timeline.
