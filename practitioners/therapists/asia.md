# Asia Therapists & Clinicians — Parental Alienation Practitioner Directory

**Schema version:** 1.0
**Last updated:** 2026-05-25
**License:** CC BY 4.0 (intended for github.com/AntiAlienate/antialienate-knowledge)
**Region:** Asia
**Countries covered:** India (IN), Singapore (SG), Hong Kong (HK), Japan (JP)
**Scope:** Asia-based clinical psychologists, psychiatric social workers, child and adolescent psychiatrists, family therapists, custody evaluators and PA-relevant academic clinicians in IN / SG / HK / JP who have (a) published peer-reviewed work explicitly framing parental alienation or alienating behaviours as a clinical phenomenon, (b) been appointed as court family specialists, custody evaluation officers, or panel experts for the local family-court system, (c) operated or directed PA-relevant reunification / co-parenting / high-conflict family interventions through a named NGO or institutional centre, or (d) issued sourced public-record statements (peer-reviewed paper, named press, conference) on PA stance. Stance is sourced, not inferred.

**Standards:** All entries verified from primary sources (national psychology registers where searchable, practice/institutional website, peer-reviewed publication PDF or PubMed/SagePub link, named press). Where a fact could not be confirmed from a public primary source it is flagged "**not publicly confirmed**". Per /CONTRIBUTING.md.

**Stance taxonomy:**
- `recognition` — treats parental alienation / 子の連れ去り / PABs as a real, assessable phenomenon; operates or endorses PA-specific reunification, expert-witness, or psycho-legal evaluation work
- `critique` — argues the PA construct is operationalised against domestic-abuse / family-violence survivors; advocates DV/trauma-informed alternatives or proscription
- `middle` — engages both alienating-behaviour and family-violence frames; differential-diagnosis approach aligned with court family specialist / family-report-writer practice
- `not_publicly_confirmed` — included for record-completeness; PA-specific stance not in primary sources

**Critical Asia regulatory & policy context (must read before using this directory):**

*India* — there is **no single national psychology licensing register**. The **Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI)** registers Clinical Psychologists under the *Rehabilitation Council of India Act 1992* via the Central Rehabilitation Register (CRR) — https://www.rehabcouncil.nic.in/ . Psychiatric Social Work is regulated by professional convention rather than statute; **NIMHANS Bengaluru** is the apex training institute and the de facto national reference centre for child mental-health forensic / custody work. The Supreme Court of India's leading PA judgment is **Lt Col Vivek Singh v Romani Singh, Civil Appeal 3962/2016, (2017) 3 SCC 231** — judgment PDF https://main.sci.gov.in/judgment/judis/44566.pdf — explicitly defining PA, anchoring the recognition camp.

*Singapore* — psychologists are **non-statutorily regulated**; the **Singapore Register of Psychologists (SRP)** is maintained by the Singapore Psychological Society — https://singaporepsychologicalsociety.org/ . The **Family Justice Courts (FJC) — Counselling and Psychological Services (CAPS)** within the FDR Division employs **Court Family Specialists (CFS)** — psychologists, counsellors and social workers — for both forensic and therapeutic work in custody, guardianship, family-violence and Youth Court matters — https://www.judiciary.gov.sg/family/counselling-psychological-services . Singapore introduced **child-access enforcement reforms (in force 2 January 2025)** via Ministry of Social and Family Development (MSF), explicitly addressing PA-adjacent breach of access orders.

*Hong Kong* — psychology is **not statutorily regulated**; multiple voluntary registers exist: **Hong Kong Psychological Society — Division of Clinical Psychology (HKPS-DCP)** https://hkps-dcp.org.hk/ ; **Hong Kong Institute of Clinical Psychologists (HKICP)** under the Accredited Registers Scheme of the Department of Health https://icphk.org.hk/ ; **Hong Kong Society for Counselling Psychology (HKSCP)** https://hkscporg.weebly.com/ ; **Hong Kong Association of Doctors in Clinical Psychology (HKADCP)** https://www.hkadcp.org.hk/ . The Social Welfare Department (SWD) operates **five Specialised Co-parenting Support Centres** (established October 2019), of which **Hong Kong Family Welfare Society's "Parent-child Connect" (PCC)** is the lead delivery agency — https://www.hkfws.org.hk/ ; SWD "Parenthood goes on" portal — https://www.swd.gov.hk/coparenting/index_e.html .

*Japan* — clinical psychology is regulated under the *Public Certified Psychologist Act* (公認心理師法, 2017) — **公認心理師 (Public Certified Psychologist)** is the national licence; the older 臨床心理士 (Certified Clinical Psychologist) credential is held by the Foundation of the Japanese Certification Board for Clinical Psychologists — http://fjcbcp.or.jp/ . Family courts employ **Family Court Investigators (家庭裁判所調査官)** rather than independent psychological expert witnesses — Guide to Family Court of Japan https://www.courts.go.jp/english/vc-files/courts-en/Material/Guide_to_the_Family_Court_of_Japan_2023.pdf . Japan **Act No. 33 of 2024** amending Civil Code Art. 819 introduced joint parental authority (共同親権) effective 1 April 2026 — the doctrinal anchor for current PA / 子の連れ去り (child abduction) debate.

## India (IN)

The Indian PA clinical terrain is anchored by a single research cluster at **NIMHANS Bengaluru** — specifically the Departments of **Psychiatric Social Work** (Jangam, Nambiar) and **Child & Adolescent Psychiatry** (Seshadri, K. John Vijay Sagar) — which has produced the only India-based peer-reviewed PA literature (Nambiar, Jangam & Seshadri 2023 *Indian J Psychol Med*; Nambiar, Jangam, Jose & Seshadri 2021 *Asian J Psychiatry*; Nambiar, Jangam & Seshadri 2024 *Indian J Psychol Med*). The institutional sibling project **SAMVAD** (Support, Advocacy & Mental health interventions for children in Vulnerable circumstances And Distress) — a Ministry of Women & Child Development national initiative co-located in NIMHANS Child & Adolescent Psychiatry — is the policy delivery arm. Outside NIMHANS, PA-specific named clinicians could not be verified from primary sources at the time of this batch; large private child-mental-health practices (Saarthak Delhi, Children First Delhi) hold no published PA stance and are listed in the Excluded section for transparency.

## Singapore (SG)

The Singapore PA clinical terrain is **structurally institutional**: the Family Justice Courts (FJC) operate an in-house **Counselling and Psychological Services (CAPS) — Court Family Specialists (CFS)** team for both forensic and therapeutic work, supplemented by **private psychologists appointed as Custody Evaluation Officers (CEOs)** and the **Singapore Register of Psychologists (SRP)** voluntary register. Singapore introduced **child-access enforcement reforms in force 2 January 2025** (Ministry of Social and Family Development) — mandatory counselling/mediation for parents breaching access orders, make-up access, and burden-of-proof shift to the care-and-control parent — which is the most explicit Asia government policy recognition of PA-adjacent harm to date. Named individual CFS officers are **not enumerated** in the FJC public register; CEOs are appointed by court order rather than via a public roster. Below are the two verified clinician/institutional entries.

## Hong Kong (HK)

The Hong Kong PA clinical terrain is structured around the **Social Welfare Department's five Specialised Co-parenting Support Centres** (established October 2019, post-2019 reform) and the multiple **voluntary psychology registers** (HKPS-DCP, HKICP, HKSCP, HKADCP). The Court of Appeal's PA-relevant anchor case is **H v W [2021] HKCA 733** (cross-referenced from the lawyer file). The lead delivery agency is the **Hong Kong Family Welfare Society (HKFWS) — Parent-child Connect (PCC)** centre, which operates the **CNCH Shared Parenting Assessment and Intervention Model** (Coordination, Negotiation, Counselling, Healing) for high-conflict separated/divorced families. The most prominent named PA-recognition clinical psychologist in the Hong Kong public record is **Dr Monica Borschel** — HKSCP-registered, BPS-Chartered, quoted in named SCMP coverage as treating PA as emotional abuse.

## Japan (JP)

The Japanese PA clinical terrain is **structurally complicated**: family courts use in-house **Family Court Investigators (家庭裁判所調査官)** rather than independent psychological expert witnesses; the **Public Certified Psychologist (公認心理師)** statutory licence (2017) and the older **Certified Clinical Psychologist (臨床心理士)** voluntary credential are the main registers; the **Japanese Association of Family Therapy (JAFT)** (founded 1984, ~1,000 members) is the national professional society. Joint parental authority (共同親権) under **Act No. 33 of 2024** entered force 1 April 2026 — the doctrinal anchor for current PA / 子の連れ去り (child abduction) debate; the recognition camp is led by NGO advocates (Joint Custody Support National Liaison Council 共同養育支援法 全国連絡会) while the **Japan Federation of Bar Associations (JFBA)** runs a DV-protection-primacy critique line. Named individual Japan-based clinical psychologists with explicit PA-specific peer-reviewed stance could not be verified within this batch's primary-sources standard; the closest verifiable named clinician is Dr Akiko Ohnogi (Tokyo expat clinical practice), included below as a flag rather than as a PA-stance entry.
