# J. Michael Bone, PhD

Licensed Mental Health Counsellor. A direct successor to Gardner and one of the most-used PA expert witnesses in US family courts.

## Why he matters here

Bone worked alongside Gardner on cases and his "Four Criteria" framework (with Walsh, 1999) for assessing alienation is a long-standing courtroom shorthand. His evaluations appear in court records nationwide; his framework is still taught in custody-evaluator CE courses. Anyone reading or contesting a US evaluator's report will benefit from understanding the framework.

## Credentials & affiliation

- PhD
- Licensed Mental Health Counsellor, Florida
- Private practice, Winter Park, Florida
- Former Special Topics Editor, *American Journal of Family Therapy*
- Former Scientific Advisory Board, Parental Alienation Research Foundation

## Key contributions

- **"Parental Alienation Syndrome: How to Detect It and What to Do About It"** (with Michael Walsh), *Florida Bar Journal*, 1999 — the "Four Criteria" framework.
- Co-author of two PA books.
- Multiple peer-reviewed chapters on PA assessment.
