# Latin American PA-recognition comparative — BR, MX, AR, CO, CL

**Jurisdiction:** Comparative · **Coverage:** Brazil · Mexico · Argentina · Colombia · Chile

A side-by-side analytical comparison of the five major Latin American PA-recognition frameworks. Latin America is the **most PA-codified region** in the world — Brazil's Lei 12.318/2010 was the first national PA-statute globally, and the framework has been adopted across Mexico's state codes (CDMX, Estado de México, Nuevo León, and six others) plus elaborated through Argentine, Colombian, and Chilean jurisprudence and statutory frameworks.

## Comparative table — Latin American PA-recognition provisions

| Jurisdiction | Express PA statute | Recognition level | Distinctive feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Brazil** | [Lei 12.318/2010](../brazil/lei-12318-2010-lei-da-alienacao-parental-verbatim.md) | **National (federal)** | First national PA-statute globally (2010); 7-tier sanction ladder |
| **Mexico (CDMX + 6 states)** | [CDMX CC art. 323 Septimus](../mexico/codigo-civil-cdmx-art-323-septimus-alienacion-parental-verbatim.md) | State (CDMX, Edomex, NL, Ags, Coa, Ver, Son) | Mild/moderate vs severe graduated remedies; classified as *violencia familiar* |
| **Argentina** | [CCyC arts. 638-655](../argentina/codigo-civil-y-comercial-2015-arts-638-655-pa-recognition.json) | National (2015 CCyC + judicial PA-recognition) | *Responsabilidad parental* + cuidado personal compartido default |
| **Colombia** | [Ley 1098/2006 (Codigo de la Infancia)](../colombia/codigo-infancia-adolescencia-ley-1098-2006.md) | National (via interés superior + ECCs) | *Custodia y cuidado personal* + ICBF integration |
| **Chile** | [CC + Ley 19.947/2004](../chile/codigo-civil-cuidado-personal-relacion-directa.md) | National (cuidado personal + relación directa y regular) | *Relación directa y regular* expressed contact framework |

## Two-tier classification by codification explicitness

### Tier 1 — Express PA-statute (definition + remedies codified)

**Brazil (Lei 12.318/2010).** The doctrinally clearest PA-statute in the world. The law expressly defines *alienação parental* and codifies a 7-tier sanction ladder. The Brazilian framework operates as the structural template for the Mexican state codes.

**Mexico (CDMX art. 323 Septimus + 6 state codes).** The most operationally graduated Latin American framework. The CDMX 2014 reform (mirrored by Estado de México, Nuevo León, Aguascalientes, Coahuila, Veracruz, Sonora) provides express definition + graduated severity ladder:
- *Leve o moderada* → guarda y custodia transfer to alienated parent + suspended visits + therapy
- *Severa* → guarda y custodia loss + no contact + may lose patria potestad + therapy

### Tier 2 — Functional PA-recognition (via jurisprudence + welfare framework)

**Argentina (CCyC 2015 arts. 638-655).** PA-recognition through the *responsabilidad parental* framework + *cuidado personal compartido* default + judicial PA-pattern doctrine. The Corte Suprema de Justicia de la Nación has developed the *interés superior del niño* + *coparentalidad* framework to address PA-pattern cases.

**Colombia (Ley 1098/2006 + Codigo Civil).** PA-recognition through the *interés superior* + *ECC* (Equipo Cooperante) framework + ICBF (Instituto Colombiano de Bienestar Familiar) integration. The Corte Constitucional has developed PA-related jurisprudence through tutela proceedings.

**Chile (CC + Ley 19.947/2004 + Ley 20.680/2013).** PA-recognition through the *cuidado personal compartido* (post-2013 reform) + *relación directa y regular* (RDR — direct and regular relationship) framework. The Tercer Tribunal de Familia line has developed RDR-protective doctrine.

## Doctrinal analysis — five key dimensions

### 1. Statutory definition

| Jurisdiction | Express definition? | Definitional elements |
|---|---|---|
| Brazil | YES — Lei 12.318 art. 2 | *Interferência* + *promoção do repúdio* + *prejuízo ao estabelecimento ou manutenção de vínculos* |
| Mexico | YES — CC CDMX art. 323 Septimus | *Conducta* + *trastorno mental* + *campañas de denigración* |
| Argentina | Implicit — via responsabilidad parental framework | Engaged via art. 640 + 655 obligations |
| Colombia | Implicit — via Ley 1098 art. 23 + interés superior | Engaged via *garantía del desarrollo armónico* |
| Chile | Implicit — via RDR framework | Engaged via *interferencia con relación directa y regular* |

Brazil and Mexico have the strongest definitional explicitness. Argentina, Colombia, and Chile rely on framework provisions interpreted by jurisprudence.

### 2. Sanction-ladder structure

**Brazil (Lei 12.318/2010 art. 6)** — 7-tier statutory sanction ladder:
- I. *Advertência* (warning)
- II. *Acompanhamento psicológico/biopsicossocial* (psychological monitoring)
- III. *Ampliação do regime de convivência* (expanded visitation for alienated parent)
- IV. *Multa* (fine)
- V. *Acompanhamento psicológico biopsicossocial obrigatório* (mandatory therapy)
- VI. *Alteração da guarda* (custody alteration)
- VII. *Suspensão da autoridade parental* (parental authority suspension)

**Mexico (CDMX art. 323 Septimus)** — 3-tier severity-based ladder:
- *Leve/moderada*: guarda transfer + suspended visits + therapy
- *Severa*: guarda loss + no contact + may lose patria potestad
- All cases: family therapy mandatory

The Brazilian 7-tier ladder is operationally more granular. The Mexican 2-tier (plus mandatory therapy across all) is structurally simpler but the *severa* tier is operationally apex (no contact + patria potestad loss).

### 3. Mandatory-therapy provisions

| Jurisdiction | Mandatory therapy? | Provider |
|---|---|---|
| Brazil | YES — Lei 12.318 art. 6 (II) and (V) | Court-appointed expert |
| Mexico | YES — art. 323 Septimus final paragraph (all cases) | DIF (Sistema Desarrollo Integral Familia) |
| Argentina | Discretionary — via art. 706 CCyC | Court-appointed *perito psicológico* |
| Colombia | Mandatory via ICBF intervention | ICBF + Defensoría de Familia |
| Chile | Mandatory via *programa terapéutico* | SENAME + ChCC psychologists |

Mexico's *DIF integration* is doctrinally distinctive — providing free public-sector access to mandated therapy. Colombia's ICBF + Defensoría de Familia framework provides parallel public-sector access.

### 4. Constitutional/Supreme Court reception

| Jurisdiction | Apex court | PA-recognition jurisprudence |
|---|---|---|
| Brazil | STJ + STF | STJ has applied Lei 12.318 in numerous decisions; STF has confirmed constitutionality |
| Mexico | SCJN | Amparo Directo en Revisión 2293/2013 confirmed PA-statute constitutionality |
| Argentina | CSJN | CSJN has developed *interés superior* + *coparentalidad* framework |
| Colombia | Corte Constitucional | T-967/2014 + tutela jurisprudence applying PA-recognition via interés superior |
| Chile | CS + TC | Tercer Tribunal de Familia line + CS reception of RDR doctrine |

### 5. Cross-border PA-enforcement (Hague 1980 reception)

All five jurisdictions are Hague 1980 signatories with developed PA-aware return jurisprudence:
- Brazil: STJ jurisprudence linking Hague return to PA-protective considerations
- Mexico: Federal jurisdiction + DIF-CDMX cooperation
- Argentina: CSJN Hague-application jurisprudence
- Colombia: ICBF Central Authority coordination
- Chile: SCJN Hague-application doctrine

## Operational implications for PA-pattern cases

### Where the framework is structurally most explicit

**Brazil (Lei 12.318/2010).** The doctrinally clearest pathway for PA-pattern cases. The 7-tier sanction ladder provides structural certainty for both judges and parties. The definitional explicitness (art. 2) means PA-conduct findings can be made directly under the statute without needing to construct framework-derivative reasoning.

**Mexico (CDMX + 6 state codes).** The most operationally graduated framework via the severity-based ladder. The *violencia familiar* classification engages the broader DV-protective legal framework alongside family-law remedies — providing structural reinforcement of the protective intervention.

### Where additional reasoning is required

**Argentina, Colombia, Chile.** PA-pattern argument is built through the welfare-framework provisions + judicially-developed PA-recognition doctrine. The frameworks are operationally effective but require more structural reasoning than the Brazilian/Mexican direct-statute pathway.

## Cross-cutting Latin American features

### 1. Welfare-paramountcy formula

All five jurisdictions converge on *interés superior del niño* (best interest of the child) — a CRC art. 3 transposition. The formulation is doctrinally strong:
- Brazil: *Melhor interesse* (best interest) under Lei 8.069/1990 (ECA — Statute of the Child and Adolescent)
- Mexico: *Interés superior* under LGDNNA art. 22
- Argentina: *Interés superior del niño* under CCyC art. 706
- Colombia: *Interés superior* under Ley 1098/2006 art. 8
- Chile: *Interés superior del niño* under Ley 19.968 (Ley de Tribunales de Familia)

### 2. Mandatory-mediation framework

All five jurisdictions have institutional mediation:
- Brazil: *Mediação familiar* under CPC + CNJ Resolution 125/2010
- Mexico: *Mediación familiar* under DIF + court-annexed
- Argentina: *Mediación familiar* obligatoria under Ley 26.589
- Colombia: *Conciliación* under Ley 640/2001 + Defensoría de Familia
- Chile: *Mediación obligatoria* under Ley 20.286

### 3. Specialised family courts

| Jurisdiction | Specialised court | Distinctive feature |
|---|---|---|
| Brazil | Varas de Família | State-level + JEC integration |
| Mexico | Juzgados Familiares | Federal entity + state-level |
| Argentina | Juzgados de Familia | Provincial + CABA |
| Colombia | Juzgados de Familia | Departmental + ECC integration |
| Chile | Tribunales de Familia | Ley 19.968 specialised network |

All five jurisdictions have specialised family-court networks, but the operational maturity varies significantly.

### 4. Public-sector family-services integration

Each jurisdiction integrates family-court proceedings with public-sector family-services:
- Brazil: CRAS (Centros de Referência de Assistência Social) + Conselho Tutelar
- Mexico: DIF (Sistema Desarrollo Integral Familia) + Procuraduría de Protección
- Argentina: SENNAF (Secretaría Nacional de Niñez) + Defensorías
- Colombia: ICBF (Instituto Colombiano de Bienestar Familiar) + Defensorías de Familia
- Chile: SENAME (Servicio Nacional de Menores) + Tribunales de Familia integration

The Colombian ICBF + Defensoría de Familia integration is doctrinally distinctive for its integrated welfare-protection + judicial-coordination structure.
