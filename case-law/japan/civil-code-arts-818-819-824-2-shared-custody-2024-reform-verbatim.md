# 民法 (Civil Code) arts. 818 + 819 + 824-2 — Joint custody + alternation framework (verbatim, 2024 reform)

**Jurisdiction:** Japan · **Citation:** *民法* (Minpō — Civil Code) arts. 818, 819, 824-2 (as amended by *Reiwa 6 Law No. 33 of 2024*) · **Authoritative source:** [e-Gov 法令検索 — 民法](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=129AC0000000089)

The **historic 2024 reform** of Japanese family law. *Reiwa 6 Law No. 33* (2024) abolished the post-divorce *sole-custody* framework that had governed Japan since 1898 — replacing it with a *joint-custody-or-sole-custody* framework where the court may order *共同親権* (kyōdō shinken — joint parental authority) over the parents' objections. The reform takes effect 2026-04-01. The provisions are the structural Japanese parallel to:
- [Korea Civil Act arts. 909-913 (joint custody post 2007 reform)](https://www.law.go.kr/)
- [Germany BGB §§ 1626 + 1671 (gemeinsame elterliche Sorge)](../germany/bgb-1626-1684-elterliche-sorge-umgang-verbatim.md)
- [France CC arts. 372-373 (autorité parentale conjointe)](../france/code-civil-371-1-377-autorite-parentale.md)
- [Italy CC art. 337-ter (affidamento condiviso)](../italy/codice-civile-art-337-ter-provvedimenti-riguardo-ai-figli.md)

Japan is doctrinally distinctive globally because the 2024 reform abolished a 126-year sole-custody tradition under direct pressure from international PA-recognition discourse and ECHR-equivalent jurisprudence on parental rights. Japan had been the major industrialized outlier maintaining post-divorce sole custody — the 2024 reform brings Japanese family law into structural alignment with the EU/US/CommonLaw shared-custody majority.

## Verbatim text (current consolidated, post-Reiwa 6 Law No. 33)

### Art. 818 民法 — 親権者

> 成年に達しない子は、父母の親権に服する。
>
> 子が養子であるときは、養親の親権に服する。
>
> 親権は、父母の婚姻中は、父母が共同して行う。ただし、父母の一方が親権を行うことができないときは、他の一方が行う。

### Art. 819 民法 — 離婚又は認知の場合の親権者

> 父母が協議上の離婚をするときは、その協議で、父母の双方又は一方を親権者と定めなければならない。
>
> 裁判上の離婚の場合には、裁判所は、父母の双方又は一方を親権者と定める。
>
> 子の出生前に父母が離婚した場合には、親権は、母が行う。ただし、子の出生後に、父母の協議で、父を親権者と定めることができる。
>
> 父が認知した子に対する親権は、父母の協議で父と母の双方又は一方を親権者と定めたときに限り、父が行う。
>
> 第一項、第三項又は前項の協議が調わないとき、又は協議をすることができないときは、家庭裁判所は、父又は母の請求によって、協議に代わる審判をすることができる。
>
> 子の利益のため必要があると認めるときは、家庭裁判所は、子の親族の請求によって、親権者を他の一方に変更することができる。

### Art. 824-2 民法 — 親権の行使方法 (post 2024 reform)

> 親権を行う父母は、共同してこれを行う。ただし、次に掲げる場合には、その一方が行うことができる。
>
> 一 他の一方が親権を行うことができないとき。
>
> 二 子の利益のため急迫の事情があるとき。
>
> 三 監護及び教育に関する日常の行為をするとき。
>
> 親権を行う父母の意見が一致しないときは、家庭裁判所は、父又は母の請求によって、親権の行使について必要な事項を定めることができる。

## English working translation

### Art. 818 — Persons exercising parental authority

> A child who has not attained the age of majority is *subject to the parental authority* of his/her *father and mother*.
>
> Where the child is an *adopted child*, he/she is subject to the parental authority of the *adoptive parents*.
>
> Parental authority is, during the *marriage* of the father and mother, *exercised jointly* by the father and mother. Where, however, *one of the parents cannot exercise parental authority*, the *other* exercises it.

### Art. 819 — Persons exercising parental authority in case of divorce or recognition

> When the father and mother *divorce by agreement*, they must determine, by their agreement, that *both* or *one* of them is the person exercising parental authority.
>
> In case of *judicial divorce*, the *court determines* that both or one of them is the person exercising parental authority.
>
> Where the father and mother divorce *before the birth of the child*, parental authority is exercised by the *mother*. Provided, however, that after the birth of the child, the parents may, by agreement, designate the father as the person exercising parental authority.
>
> [Provisions on recognized children]
>
> Where the agreement under paragraphs 1, 3, or the preceding paragraph *cannot be reached* or *cannot be made*, the *Family Court* may, *upon application of the father or mother*, *make an adjudication in lieu of the agreement*.
>
> *Where it finds it necessary for the welfare of the child*, the Family Court may, upon application of *the child's relatives*, *change* the person exercising parental authority to the *other parent*.

### Art. 824-2 — Method of exercise of parental authority (post-2024)

> The father and mother who exercise parental authority *exercise it jointly* (*共同してこれを行う*). Provided, however, that in the following cases, *one* of them may exercise it:
>
> 1. Where *the other cannot exercise* parental authority;
>
> 2. Where there are *urgent circumstances* (*急迫の事情*) for the welfare of the child;
>
> 3. When making *acts of daily monitoring and education* (*監護及び教育に関する日常の行為*).
>
> Where the *opinions of the father and mother exercising parental authority do not coincide*, the Family Court may, upon application of the father or mother, *determine the necessary matters* concerning the exercise of parental authority.

## Why this matters for PA

- **The historic shift from sole to joint custody (art. 819 + 824-2).** Before the 2024 reform, art. 819 *required* that one parent be designated sole custodian post-divorce — Japan was the major industrialized outlier on this. The 2024 reform replaced the *one-of-them* mandatory framework with a *both-or-one* framework, expressly authorizing joint custody (*共同親権 kyōdō shinken*). In PA-pattern cases this is structurally transformative: the alienating parent's previous near-monopoly on post-divorce custody is no longer guaranteed.
- **The court's adjudication-in-lieu power (art. 819 paragraph 5).** Where parents cannot reach agreement, the *Family Court may make an adjudication in lieu of the agreement*. This is doctrinally important because it provides structural pathway for joint custody where the alienating parent refuses agreement.
- **The change-of-custodian power (art. 819 paragraph 6).** *Where it finds it necessary for the welfare of the child, the Family Court may change the person exercising parental authority to the other parent.* The change-of-custodian provision applies regardless of whether the original allocation was joint or sole. In PA-pattern cases this provides the structural transfer remedy.
- **The relatives' standing (art. 819 paragraph 6).** *Upon application of the child's relatives.* Doctrinally distinctive — the change-of-custodian application can be brought by the child's *relatives*, not only the targeted parent. This provides structural pathway for grandparents or other family members to seek intervention where the targeted parent is unable to litigate effectively.
- **The joint-exercise default (art. 824-2 first paragraph).** *親権を行う父母は、共同してこれを行う.* The parents exercising parental authority *exercise it jointly*. This is the structural starting point post-2024 reform. In PA-pattern cases this means the alienating parent's unilateral decisions on enumerated matters are not lawfully authorized — joint exercise is the default.
- **The three exceptions (art. 824-2 first paragraph exceptions).** Joint exercise admits three exceptions:
  - When the other cannot exercise (incapacity)
  - *急迫の事情* (urgent circumstances) for the child's welfare
  - *監護及び教育に関する日常の行為* (acts of daily monitoring and education)
- The *急迫の事情* exception is the structural emergency provision; the *日常の行為* exception preserves day-to-day decision-making by the cohabiting parent. In PA-pattern cases the *急迫の事情* exception requires evidenced urgency — alienating-parent invocation of this exception for non-urgent matters constitutes overreach.
- **The Family Court adjudication power for disagreement (art. 824-2 second paragraph).** *父又は母の請求によって、親権の行使について必要な事項を定めることができる.* Upon application of the father or mother, the Family Court may *determine the necessary matters* concerning the exercise of parental authority. This is doctrinally distinctive — the court is the structural arbiter of disagreements, not the parties unilaterally. In PA-pattern cases this provides express pathway for the targeted parent to invoke court adjudication where the alienating parent refuses joint exercise.
- **The pre-birth divorce framework (art. 819 paragraph 3).** *父母が離婚した場合には、親権は、母が行う.* Pre-birth divorce produces maternal custody by default. This is doctrinally significant in PA-pattern cases where pre-natal separation occurred — but the *父母の協議で、父を親権者と定めることができる* (father may be designated by parental agreement post-birth) provision preserves structural flexibility.
- **The international PA-discourse context.** The 2024 reform was driven by sustained international pressure including:
  - The European Parliament resolution of 2020-07-08 on the international and domestic parental abduction of EU children in Japan
  - The French Senate resolution of 2020-02-05
  - Sustained ECHR jurisprudence on parental rights
  - Domestic *kobetsu mondai* (kidnapping problem) advocacy by left-behind parents
- The 2024 reform's preparatory documents expressly acknowledged the PA-discourse context, providing operational interpretive guidance for treating PA-pattern conduct as engaging the welfare-protective framework.
- **The transitional implementation issues.** The reform's 2026-04-01 effective date creates significant transitional questions about:
  - Pre-reform sole-custody arrangements (whether and how they can be revised)
  - Cross-border PA-pattern cases (Hague 1980 + Japan's particular jurisprudence)
  - Family-court capacity to absorb the new caseload
- Japanese family-court practice is expected to continue developing through 2026-2028 as the reform's operational framework consolidates.

## Procedural application

- The competent court is the *家庭裁判所* (Kateisaibansho — Family Court) under [家事事件手続法](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=423AC0000000052) (Domestic Relations Case Procedure Act 2011)
- *家庭裁判所調査官* (Family Court Probation Officer) provides welfare assessment
- The child is heard under the *家事事件手続法* art. 65 + art. 152 — capacity-based standard, typically age 10+ for direct hearing
- *家事調停* (family mediation) is structurally available and frequently engaged pre-adjudication
- Appellate review to the *高等裁判所* (High Court) + cassation to the *最高裁判所* (Supreme Court)

## Use in alienating-conduct cases

- The pre-reform sole-custody framework had been structurally incompatible with PA-protection — the alienating parent's monopoly on post-divorce custody was near-total
- The 2024 reform's joint-custody framework provides structural transformation, but operational practice will develop through 2026-2028
- The Saikō saibansho (Supreme Court) is expected to develop the *子の利益* (child's welfare) + *kyōdō shinken* framework in PA-pattern cases
- Where alienating conduct is established post-reform, Japanese courts may:
  - First-tier: order *家事調停* (mediation) under DRCPA
  - Second-tier: fix specific 共同親権 arrangements under art. 819
  - Third-tier: order change-of-custodian under art. 819 paragraph 6
  - Apex-tier: 親権喪失 (loss of parental authority) under art. 834
- The *面会交流* (menkai kōryū — contact/visitation) framework operates alongside the parental-authority provisions, with structural enforcement via *履行勧告* (rikō kankoku — performance encouragement) and *間接強制* (kansetsu kyōsei — indirect compulsion)
