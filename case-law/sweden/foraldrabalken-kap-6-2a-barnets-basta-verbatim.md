# Föräldrabalken 6 kap. 2a § — Barnets bästa (verbatim)

**Jurisdiction:** Sweden · **Citation:** *Föräldrabalken* 6 kap. 2a § · **Authoritative source:** [riksdagen.se — Föräldrabalken 6 kap.](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/foraldrabalk-1949381_sfs-1949-381/)

The **welfare paramountcy + child's voice + risk factors** in Swedish family law. Föräldrabalken 6 kap. 2a § establishes that *barnets bästa* (the child's best interests) shall be the decisive consideration in all questions concerning custody (*vårdnad*), residence (*boende*), and contact (*umgänge*). The provision codifies three structural elements: (i) the paramountcy formulation, (ii) the risk-of-harm consideration, and (iii) the child's wishes consideration tied to age and maturity.

The provision is the Swedish structural parallel to:
- [Germany BGB § 1697a Kindeswohlprinzip](../germany/bgb-1697a-kindeswohlprinzip-verbatim.md)
- [UK Children Act 1989 s. 1 welfare paramountcy](../united-kingdom/children-act-1989-section-1.md)
- [Ireland Guardianship of Infants Act 1964 s. 3](../ireland/guardianship-of-infants-act-1964-s3-welfare-paramountcy-verbatim.md)
- [Norway barneloven § 48](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1981-04-08-7/§48)

Sweden is doctrinally distinctive in European welfare-paramountcy law because the 2006 reform expressly imported the *risk för skada* (risk of harm) factor into the welfare-paramountcy provision itself — making it operationally the structural starting point of welfare assessment, not a counter-consideration to contact.

## Verbatim text (current consolidated)

### 6 kap. 2a § Föräldrabalken — Barnets bästa

> Barnets bästa skall vara avgörande för alla frågor om vårdnad, boende och umgänge.
>
> Vid bedömningen av vad som är bäst för barnet skall det fästas avseende särskilt vid
>
> – risken för att barnet eller någon annan i familjen utsätts för övergrepp eller att barnet olovligen förs bort eller hålls kvar eller annars far illa, och
>
> – barnets behov av en nära och god kontakt med båda föräldrarna.
>
> Hänsyn skall tas till barnets vilja med beaktande av barnets ålder och mognad.

## English working translation

### 6 kap. 2a § Parental Code — Best interests of the child

> The best interests of the child shall be *decisive* (*avgörande*) for all questions concerning custody, residence, and contact.
>
> In assessing what is best for the child, particular regard shall be had to:
>
> – the *risk* that the child or someone else in the family is subjected to *abuse* (*övergrepp*), or that the child is *unlawfully removed or retained* or otherwise *comes to harm* (*far illa*), and
>
> – the child's need for a *close and good contact* (*nära och god kontakt*) with both parents.
>
> *Regard shall be had* to the child's *wishes* (*vilja*) with consideration to the child's *age and maturity* (*ålder och mognad*).

## Why this matters for PA

- **Avgörande paramountcy formulation.** *Barnets bästa skall vara avgörande.* The child's best interests shall be *decisive* (avgörande). This is one of the strongest welfare-paramountcy formulations in European family law — *avgörande* is doctrinally stronger than "paramount consideration" (UK/IE), "leitender Gesichtspunkt" (AT), or "am besten entspricht" (DE). The Swedish formulation positions the child's welfare as the controlling decisional standard, not merely first among considerations.
- **The risk-factor codification (first dash).** *Risken för att barnet eller någon annan i familjen utsätts för övergrepp eller att barnet olovligen förs bort eller hålls kvar eller annars far illa.* The risk of:
  - *Övergrepp* (abuse) — to the child or *anyone else in the family*
  - *Olovligen förs bort eller hålls kvar* (unlawful removal or retention)
  - *Far illa* (comes to harm) generally
- This is doctrinally distinctive — the welfare paramountcy itself incorporates the risk-of-harm factor, including risk to *other family members*. In PA-pattern cases this is critical: the alienating parent's conduct toward the targeted parent (psychological abuse, coercive control) engages the first dash *to the same degree* as conduct toward the child.
- **The both-parent-contact codification (second dash).** *Barnets behov av en nära och god kontakt med båda föräldrarna.* The child's need for a *close and good contact* with *both parents*. This is the structural Swedish equivalent of:
  - Austrian [§ 138 (1) Z 9 verlässliche Kontakte](../austria/abgb-138-kindeswohl-checklist-verbatim.md)
  - UK [s. 1(2A) presumption of involvement](../united-kingdom/children-act-1989-section-1.md)
  - Italian [art. 337-ter bigenitorialità](../italy/codice-civile-art-337-ter-provvedimenti-riguardo-ai-figli.md)
- The *både föräldrarna* (both parents) clause is the doctrinal anchor for the structural welfare interest in both-parent contact in Swedish law.
- **The balance between dashes 1 and 2.** The two factors operate as structural balancers — the risk-of-harm factor (dash 1) does *not* automatically override the both-parent-contact factor (dash 2). The 2006 reform was followed by jurisprudential development distinguishing actual risk from speculative or aggregated risk. In PA-pattern cases the alienating parent's framing of contact as "harmful" must be tested against the *concrete and current* risk standard developed in the case law.
- **The child's wishes consideration.** *Hänsyn skall tas till barnets vilja med beaktande av barnets ålder och mognad.* Regard *shall* be had to the child's wishes, *with consideration to age and maturity*. This is the capacity-based standard — no fixed age threshold. The 2024 Swedish reform implementations have reinforced that the child's *vilja* must be tested for autonomy of formation, especially in PA-pattern cases where the child's expressed wishes may be the product of alienating influence.
- **The 2022 strängare syn på våld reform.** The 2022 reform tightened the violence-screening provisions throughout Föräldrabalken. The first-dash risk factor was operationally strengthened — domestic violence findings now structurally engage the risk consideration with heightened weight. In PA-pattern cases this has the doctrinal consequence that DV allegations *and* PA-pattern findings can both engage the welfare paramountcy under the same structural analysis.

## Procedural application

- The competent court is the *tingsrätt* (district court), then *hovrätten* (court of appeal), then the *Högsta domstolen* (Supreme Court)
- *Socialtjänsten* (social services) provides welfare assessment under chap. 6 § 19
- *Vårdnadsutredning* (custody investigation) is the formal welfare-assessment instrument
- The child is heard under chap. 6 § 2b — capacity-based standard
- The 2022 reform introduced *informationssamtal* (information meetings) as a structural intervention before adversarial litigation

## Use in alienating-conduct cases

- The Högsta domstolen has confirmed the operational structure of the avgörande paramountcy — see *NJA 2007 s. 382* and following authorities
- The hovrätten line on PA-pattern cases includes *RH 2018:42* and subsequent decisions developing the *föräldrakonflikt* (parental conflict) framework
- The 2022 reform jurisprudence has continued to develop the structural balance between dashes 1 and 2 — sustained alienating conduct can engage the first dash via psychological abuse and *far illa* findings
- Where the alienating parent's conduct is established, the second dash provides the structural foundation for contact-protective remedies including *boendebyte* (residence transfer)
- Where the child's *vilja* under chap. 6 § 2a third paragraph is the product of alienating influence, Swedish jurisprudence requires assessment of autonomy of formation — the wishes are not dispositive
