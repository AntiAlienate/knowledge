# Codice Civile art. 337-bis — Ambito di applicazione

> **TL;DR.** Italian Codice Civile article 337-bis is the **procedural-trigger article** for the entire Capo II framework (articles 337-bis through 337-octies) governing custody/contact decisions in the separation, divorce, dissolution, annulment, and unmarried-parent contexts. It is a single-sentence article: "In case of separation, dissolution, cessation of civil effects, annulment, nullity of marriage and in proceedings concerning children born outside marriage, the provisions of this Chapter apply." Every other PA-relevant Italian custody provision — joint-custody-default (337-ter), exclusive custody (337-quater), measures in case of breach (337-quinquies), and the listening of the child (337-octies) — is engaged via the article 337-bis trigger.

**Source:** brocardi.it canonical — Italian state statute, public domain. · **Fetched:** 2026-06-13 · **Editorial commentary licence:** [CC BY 4.0](../../LICENSE)

## Full statutory text — verbatim Italian

### Art. 337-bis — Ambito di applicazione

In caso di separazione, scioglimento, cessazione degli effetti civili, annullamento, nullità del matrimonio e nei procedimenti relativi ai figli nati fuori del matrimonio si applicano le disposizioni del presente capo.

---

## English translation (working — not an official translation)

### Article 337-bis — Scope of application

In case of separation, dissolution, cessation of civil effects, annulment, nullity of marriage and in proceedings concerning children born outside marriage, the provisions of this Chapter apply.

---

## Why article 337-bis matters

Article 337-bis is the **entry point** to the Italian framework governing custody and contact decisions in all family-law contexts where the parents' relationship has terminated or where the child was born outside marriage. It establishes the application of capo II (articles 337-bis through 337-octies) to the following situations:

- **Separation** (separazione) — both consensual and judicial (separazione consensuale + separazione giudiziale).
- **Dissolution / cessation of civil effects** (scioglimento + cessazione degli effetti civili) — the Italian terminology for divorce (the former for civil marriages, the latter for religious marriages dissolved civilly).
- **Annulment / nullity of marriage** (annullamento + nullità del matrimonio) — judicial findings that the marriage was either voidable (annullamento) or never legally constituted (nullità).
- **Proceedings concerning children born outside marriage** (procedimenti relativi ai figli nati fuori del matrimonio) — substantively equalised by the 2012 Riforma Filiazione (D.Lgs. 154/2013); the article 337-bis trigger ensures uniform application of capo II provisions regardless of marital status of the parents.

The substantive provisions engaged via the 337-bis trigger are:

- **Art. 337-ter** — Provvedimenti riguardo ai figli (the joint-custody-default + bigenitoriality + non-compliance modification trigger statute);
- **Art. 337-quater** — Affidamento ad un solo genitore (the exclusive-custody residual exception);
- **Art. 337-quinquies** — Revisione delle disposizioni concernenti l'affidamento dei figli (revision-of-arrangements provision);
- **Art. 337-sexies** — Assegnazione della casa familiare (allocation of the family home);
- **Art. 337-septies** — Disposizioni in favore dei figli maggiorenni (provisions for adult children);
- **Art. 337-octies** — Poteri del giudice e ascolto del minore (court powers + child hearing — the mandatory listening of the minor aged 12+ or younger if capable of discernment).

The procedural rule that all of these apply uniformly via the 337-bis trigger is operationally important — it eliminates substantive distinctions between marital and non-marital parental conflict for purposes of the custody-and-contact analysis.
