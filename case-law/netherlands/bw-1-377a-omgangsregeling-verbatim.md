# Burgerlijk Wetboek 1:377a — Recht op omgang (verbatim)

**Jurisdiction:** Netherlands · **Citation:** *Burgerlijk Wetboek* Boek 1, art. 377a · **Authoritative source:** [wetten.overheid.nl — art. 1:377a BW](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002656/2024-01-01/0/Boek1/Titeldeel15/Afdeling2/Artikel377a)

The **contact-right provision** in Dutch family law. BW art. 1:377a establishes the child's reciprocal right to *omgang* (contact) with both parents — irrespective of which parent has ouderlijk gezag (parental responsibility) — and codifies the four exhaustive grounds (*ontzeggingsgronden*) on which the court may refuse or restrict contact. The provision is the Dutch structural parallel to:
- [Germany BGB § 1684 (Umgangsrecht + Wohlverhaltensklausel)](../germany/bgb-1626-1684-elterliche-sorge-umgang-verbatim.md)
- [Austria ABGB § 187 (Kontaktrecht)](../austria/abgb-159-187-wohlverhaltensgebot-kontaktrecht-verbatim.md)
- [France Code civil art. 373-2 + 373-2-1 (droit aux relations personnelles)](../france/code-civil-371-1-377-autorite-parentale.md)
- [Italy Codice Civile art. 337-ter (provvedimenti riguardo ai figli)](../italy/codice-civile-art-337-ter-provvedimenti-riguardo-ai-figli.md)

Article 1:377a is doctrinally distinctive in Dutch law because the four ontzeggingsgronden in subsection (3) operate as a *closed list* — contact cannot be denied on grounds outside the enumerated four. This is structurally stronger than the open-ended welfare-paramountcy frameworks of UK and Ireland.

## Verbatim text (current consolidated)

### Artikel 1:377a BW — Recht op omgang

> **1.** Het kind heeft het recht op omgang met zijn ouders en met degene die in een nauwe persoonlijke betrekking tot hem staat. De niet met het gezag belaste ouder heeft het recht op en de verplichting tot omgang met zijn kind.
>
> **2.** De rechter stelt op verzoek van de ouders of van een van hen of degene die in een nauwe persoonlijke betrekking staat tot het kind, al dan niet voor bepaalde tijd, een regeling inzake de uitoefening van het omgangsrecht vast dan wel ontzegt, al dan niet voor bepaalde tijd, het recht op omgang.
>
> **3.** De rechter ontzegt het recht op omgang slechts, indien:
>
> a. omgang ernstig nadeel zou opleveren voor de geestelijke of lichamelijke ontwikkeling van het kind, of
>
> b. de ouder of degene die in een nauwe persoonlijke betrekking staat tot het kind kennelijk ongeschikt of kennelijk niet in staat moet worden geacht tot omgang, of
>
> c. het kind dat twaalf jaren of ouder is, bij zijn verhoor van ernstige bezwaren tegen omgang met zijn ouder of met degene met wie hij in een nauwe persoonlijke betrekking staat heeft doen blijken, of
>
> d. omgang anderszins in strijd is met zwaarwegende belangen van het kind.

## English working translation

### Article 1:377a BW — Right to contact

> **1.** The child has the right to contact with his/her parents and with the person who stands in a close personal relationship to him/her. The parent who is not entrusted with parental responsibility (*gezag*) has the right and the obligation to contact with his/her child.
>
> **2.** The court — upon application by the parents, or one of them, or the person standing in a close personal relationship to the child — fixes, for a determinate time or otherwise, an arrangement for the exercise of the right to contact, or refuses, for a determinate time or otherwise, the right to contact.
>
> **3.** The court refuses the right to contact only where:
>
> a. contact would cause *serious detriment* (*ernstig nadeel*) to the mental or physical development of the child, or
>
> b. the parent or person in close personal relationship to the child must be considered *manifestly unsuitable* (*kennelijk ongeschikt*) or *manifestly incapable* (*kennelijk niet in staat*) of contact, or
>
> c. the child who is *twelve years or older* has, on being heard, made apparent *serious objections* (*ernstige bezwaren*) to contact with the parent or person in close personal relationship to him/her, or
>
> d. contact is otherwise contrary to *weighty interests of the child* (*zwaarwegende belangen van het kind*).

## Why this matters for PA

- **The child's right (subsection 1).** *Het kind heeft het recht op omgang met zijn ouders.* The child *has the right* to contact with both parents — this is doctrinally significant because it frames the contact right as a *child's right* (not solely a parental right). This framing protects against the alienating parent's framing of contact denial as the child's choice — contact is the child's *right*, not the child's discretionary choice.
- **The non-resident parent's right AND obligation (subsection 1).** *De niet met het gezag belaste ouder heeft het recht op en de verplichting tot omgang.* The non-resident parent has *both* the right *and the obligation* to contact. This is doctrinally distinctive — Dutch law codifies the welfare-protective principle that the targeted parent has a *duty* to maintain contact, not merely a right. In PA-pattern cases this means the targeted parent cannot be passive in the face of alienation — they must actively pursue contact.
- **The closed list of ontzeggingsgronden (subsection 3).** *De rechter ontzegt het recht op omgang slechts, indien…* The court refuses contact *only* where one of the four grounds is established. This is the structurally distinctive feature of Dutch law — contact can be denied *only* on these four grounds, not on general welfare-paramountcy grounds. This provides stronger structural protection for contact than open-ended welfare frameworks.
- **Ground (a) — ernstig nadeel.** *Serious detriment* to mental or physical development. The *ernstig* (serious) qualifier is a high threshold — mere welfare-suboptimality is insufficient. Hoge Raad jurisprudence has reinforced that contact-denial requires concrete, evidenced detriment, not speculative or aggregated harm.
- **Ground (b) — kennelijk ongeschikt + kennelijk niet in staat.** *Manifestly unsuitable* or *manifestly incapable*. The *kennelijk* (manifestly) qualifier is similarly high — the unsuitability or incapacity must be evident, not merely arguable. In PA-pattern cases this is rarely satisfied where the targeted parent's only "incapacity" is the alienated relationship.
- **Ground (c) — twaalf jaren or older + ernstige bezwaren.** The *age-twelve* threshold for the child's bezwaren (objections) to operate as an independent contact-denial ground. This is doctrinally distinctive: below age 12 the child's expressed objections are evidential input but not an independent ontzeggingsgrond. Above age 12 the objections must be *ernstig* (serious) and revealed through formal hearing.
- **The PA-pattern significance of ground (c).** In sustained PA cases the alienating parent typically positions the child to express objections — but ground (c) requires that the objections be *ernstig* and the product of the child's autonomous formation. Hoge Raad jurisprudence (HR 8 December 2000; HR 17 January 2014) has developed the doctrine that the court must investigate the *source* of the child's stated objections — alienating-influence findings can defeat ground (c) even where the child expresses objections.
- **Ground (d) — zwaarwegende belangen residual.** *Otherwise contrary to weighty interests of the child.* This is the structural residual ground — the catch-all for welfare-protective contact-denial. The *zwaarwegende* (weighty) qualifier means general welfare-suboptimality is insufficient; the interests must be substantial. In PA-pattern cases ground (d) is rarely engaged because the welfare-protective interest typically supports rather than defeats contact.

## Procedural application

- The proceedings are heard by the *rechtbank* (district court) family chamber
- Onset by application from a parent or person in close personal relationship under subsection (2)
- The *Raad voor de Kinderbescherming* (Child Protection Board) provides welfare assessment
- The child age 12+ is heard under [art. 809 Wetboek van Burgerlijke Rechtsvordering](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0001827/2024-01-01/0/Tweedeboek/Titel6/Afdeling6) — but the court may hear younger children at its discretion
- Appellate review to the *gerechtshof* + cassatie to the *Hoge Raad* are available

## Use in alienating-conduct cases

- Hoge Raad has developed the *onderzoeksplicht* (duty to investigate) doctrine — where the child's stated objections under ground (c) may be the product of alienating influence, the court must investigate the source
- The Hof Den Haag and Hof Arnhem-Leeuwarden have developed parallel ouderverstoting (parental alienation) doctrine treating sustained alienating conduct as engaging ground (a) — *ernstig nadeel to the child's mental development* operates through the alienation-pattern psychological harm framework
- The targeted parent's *verplichting tot omgang* in subsection (1) is doctrinally important — the court can decline to find ground (b) (kennelijk ongeschikt) where the targeted parent has actively but unsuccessfully pursued contact
- Where contact is restricted under subsection (2), the court typically orders *begeleide omgang* (supervised contact) through the [Omgangshuis](https://www.omgangshuis.nl/) network as an intermediate measure before consideration of full ontzegging
