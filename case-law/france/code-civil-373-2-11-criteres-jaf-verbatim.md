# Code civil art. 373-2-11 — Critères du JAF (verbatim, 6 statutory factors)

**Jurisdiction:** France · **Citation:** *Code civil* art. 373-2-11 · **Authoritative source:** [legifrance.gouv.fr — art. 373-2-11 Code civil](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006426849)

The **statutory criteria checklist** that the *juge aux affaires familiales* (JAF) applies when ruling on the modalities of *autorité parentale* — residence, contact, and decision-making allocation. Article 373-2-11 codifies the six factors the JAF *takes particular care to consider* (*prend notamment en considération*) in fixing the arrangements. The provision is the French structural parallel to:
- [Austria ABGB § 138 Kindeswohl-Checklist](../austria/abgb-138-kindeswohl-checklist-verbatim.md)
- [UK Children Act 1989 s. 1(3) welfare checklist](../united-kingdom/children-act-1989-section-1.md)
- [Ireland Guardianship of Infants Act 1964 Part V welfare checklist](../ireland/guardianship-of-infants-act-1964-s3-welfare-paramountcy-verbatim.md)

Article 373-2-11 is doctrinally distinctive among the welfare-checklist provisions because two of its enumerated factors — items 4 (results of expert assessments, particularly on the age of the child) and 5 (information gathered on possible *pressions ou violences* exercised on the child or the other parent) — are *expressly* relevant to PA-pattern analysis. The French framework codifies the *pressions* (pressures) concept that other jurisdictions treat only as implicit considerations.

## Verbatim text (current consolidated)

### Article 373-2-11 du Code civil — Critères du JAF

> Lorsqu'il se prononce sur les modalités d'exercice de l'autorité parentale, le juge prend notamment en considération :
>
> 1° La pratique que les parents avaient précédemment suivie ou les accords qu'ils avaient pu antérieurement conclure ;
>
> 2° Les sentiments exprimés par l'enfant mineur dans les conditions prévues à l'article 388-1 ;
>
> 3° L'aptitude de chacun des parents à assumer ses devoirs et respecter les droits de l'autre ;
>
> 4° Le résultat des expertises éventuellement effectuées, tenant compte notamment de l'âge de l'enfant ;
>
> 5° Les renseignements qui ont été recueillis dans les éventuelles enquêtes et contre-enquêtes sociales prévues à l'article 373-2-12 ;
>
> 6° Les pressions ou violences, à caractère physique ou psychologique, exercées par l'un des parents sur la personne de l'autre.

## English working translation

### Article 373-2-11 Code civil — JAF criteria

> When ruling on the modalities of the exercise of parental authority, the judge takes into particular consideration:
>
> 1° The practice the parents had previously followed or the agreements they had been able to conclude previously;
>
> 2° The sentiments expressed by the minor child under the conditions provided for in article 388-1;
>
> 3° The aptitude of each parent to assume his/her duties and to respect the rights of the other;
>
> 4° The results of expert assessments that may have been carried out, taking into account in particular the age of the child;
>
> 5° The information gathered in any social investigations and counter-investigations provided for in article 373-2-12;
>
> 6° The pressures or violence, of a physical or psychological nature, exercised by one of the parents upon the person of the other.

## Why this matters for PA

- **Statutory checklist + welfare paramountcy.** Article 373-2-11 operates alongside [article 373-2-6](code-civil-371-1-377-autorite-parentale.md) (general welfare framework) and [article 373-2-9](code-civil-371-1-377-autorite-parentale.md) (residence framework). The checklist is *not* exhaustive (*notamment* — particularly) but the six enumerated factors are the structural anchors the JAF must address in reasoning.
- **Factor 3 — aptitude à respecter les droits de l'autre.** *L'aptitude de chacun des parents à assumer ses devoirs et respecter les droits de l'autre.* The aptitude of each parent to assume duties *and respect the rights of the other parent*. This is the French doctrinal foundation of the *parent coopératif* (cooperative parent) principle — the JAF expressly assesses each parent's willingness to facilitate the other parent's exercise of *autorité parentale*. Sustained alienating conduct directly engages factor 3.
- **Factor 5 — enquêtes sociales.** *Les renseignements qui ont été recueillis dans les éventuelles enquêtes et contre-enquêtes sociales.* Information gathered in *enquêtes sociales* (social investigations) — the multidisciplinary welfare-assessment framework analogous to the German Familiengerichtshilfe and the Austrian Familiengerichtshilfe. In PA-pattern cases the enquête sociale is the principal evidential vehicle for establishing the conduct pattern.
- **Factor 6 — pressions ou violences psychologiques.** *Les pressions ou violences, à caractère physique ou psychologique, exercées par l'un des parents sur la personne de l'autre.* Pressures or violence, *physical or psychological*, exercised by one parent on the person of the other. This is the doctrinally distinctive French provision: the express codification of *psychological pressures* as a JAF criterion. Psychological pressure on the targeted parent is direct welfare-relevant evidence under factor 6 — the *contrôle coercitif* (coercive control) framework French jurisprudence has developed maps onto this factor.
- **Factor 2 — sentiments de l'enfant + art. 388-1.** *Les sentiments exprimés par l'enfant mineur dans les conditions prévues à l'article 388-1.* The expressed view of the minor child, taken under the article 388-1 audition framework. Factor 2 codifies the structural channel for the child's voice — but the cross-reference to art. 388-1 means the assessment is bounded by the *discernement* (discernment) standard of that provision, not a fixed age threshold.
- **Factor 4 — expertises.** *Le résultat des expertises éventuellement effectuées, tenant compte notamment de l'âge de l'enfant.* Expert assessments, particularly with regard to the *âge de l'enfant*. In PA cases this is the structural channel for child-and-family-systems expert evidence — Cassation has confirmed that the JAF must reason with reference to the expertise findings where relied upon.
- **Factor 1 — pratique antérieure + accords.** *La pratique que les parents avaient précédemment suivie ou les accords qu'ils avaient pu antérieurement conclure.* Past parenting practice and prior agreements. This is doctrinally important in PA-pattern cases because it provides the structural anchor for documenting departure from a previously functional arrangement — sustained alienation often manifests as departure from an established practice.

## Procedural application

- Article 373-2-11 operates as the structural framework for JAF reasoning on residence/contact modalities
- The order must address the relevant factors with reasoning specific to the case
- The *enquête sociale* under art. 373-2-12 provides the principal evidential foundation
- The audition of the child under [art. 388-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006450826) feeds into factor 2
- Cour de cassation has held that the JAF's failure to address the relevant factors is reversible error — see [Cass civ 1ère 22 mars 2023](cass-civ-1ere-22-mars-2023.md)

## Use in alienating-conduct cases

- Factors 3 + 6 together provide the structural foundation for PA-pattern analysis under French law
- Factor 3 (*aptitude à respecter les droits de l'autre*) is engaged where sustained alienating conduct demonstrates inability or unwillingness to facilitate the other parent's exercise of autorité parentale
- Factor 6 (*pressions psychologiques*) is engaged where the alienating parent's conduct toward the targeted parent constitutes psychological pressure or coercion
- The interaction with [art. 373-2-1 alinéa 5 (transferable residence)](code-civil-371-1-377-autorite-parentale.md) means the JAF can use the factor 3 finding as the welfare-protective foundation for residence reallocation
- Where factor 6 findings establish coercive control, the JAF can decline shared residence even where it would otherwise be the welfare-preferred arrangement (loi 2024 reform)
